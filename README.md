# **Upper Airway Model for Endotracheal Intubation**

## _Overview_

This project's primary purpose is to be an anatomically accurate model of the upper airway that can be 3D printed for research projects.
The model is currently in the process of being developed on Blender by creating our own models, as well as downloading models from reliable websites such as NIH.
The main parts of the upper airway that will be included are the nose and nasal cavity, mouth and oral cavity, pharynx, and trachea. 
The esophagus and the digestive tract will also be included to test tubes going down the trachea rather than the esophagus. 

## _Key Anatomical Structures_

 - External nose
 - Nasal cavity
 - Nasal septum
 - Oral cavity
 - Hard palate
 - Soft palate
 - Tongue
 - Oropharynx
 - Nasopharynx
 - Laryngopharynx (hypopharynx)
 - Epiglottis
 - Laryngeal inlet
 - Vocal cords (simplified)
 - Trachea
 - Esophagus

## _Project Applications_

 -  3D Printing for research projects 
 -  Further CAD design for structures such as the lung or stomach
 -  Educational purposes for visualization of anatomical structures
